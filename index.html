How to play
  1. Draw a card, then select the active endpoint and a matching station. A Joker matches any station.
  2. Draw straight or 45° diagonal segments. Avoid crossings, reused segments, intermediate stations and loops.
  3. A Switch allows one branch from a station on your current line. Skip a card if you cannot use it.
  4. Play up to eight used cards per line. At five cards of one type, use End Round to move on.
