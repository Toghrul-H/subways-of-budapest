Subways of Budapest

Build neon metro lines across Buda and Pest.

Game Rules
  • 4 rounds (M1–M4, random order)
  • 8 cards per round (A–D + Jokers)
  • Start from your line's start station, connect valid pairs
  • Segments must be straight or 45° diagonal
  • No intersections or loops
